    Open DocuSign
    1. Open the PDF and check every page.
    2. Sign and date it on your last day worked. The AHS guide describes DocuSign: sign in with your AHS account, choose Start → Sign a Document, upload the PDF, and add Signature and Date Signed. Download AHS signing instructions.
    3. Follow the form’s supervisor/reviewer signature requirement, including the hours-difference confirmation if applicable.
    4. Attach the signed copy to your email to pnpptimestudies@alamedahealthsystem.org. The buttons below prepare email text; they do not attach or send your form.

    The signature block also confirms training before the study. The notice separately gives December 31, 2026 as the annual learning-module deadline. Contact PNPPTimeStudies if your training status is unclear.
